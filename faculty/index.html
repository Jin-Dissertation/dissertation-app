AI-Assisted Multiple Choice Question Writing App

Faculty Version

ⓘTraining tip

These “Training tip” boxes explain the workflow as you move through the app.

This version is designed for faculty who would like a structured set of prompts for using AI to write and refine multiple-choice questions. Choose the teaching context that best fits your work, add a little information about the course, then copy the prompts into the AI tool you prefer.

Your entries stay in this page. This version does not send or save your course information, prompt choices, or question-writing activity.

Nursing faculty interested in the study:
If you are a prelicensure nursing faculty member and are interested in participating in the research study this tool was originally developed for, email tjin@crimson.ua.edu.
