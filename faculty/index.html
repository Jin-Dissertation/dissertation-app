AI-Assisted Question Writing Tool

Build, refine, audit, and finalize multiple-choice questions using prompts you can copy into the AI tool of your choice.

No login · No data collection · No backend connection
Nursing faculty: This tool grew out of a dissertation study on AI-assisted question generation in nursing education. If you are interested in participating in that study, email tjin@crimson.ua.edu.

A. Teaching context

Choose the option that best matches your teaching. This changes a few prompts so the question guidance fits your field.

Tip: The four modes use the same basic workflow. Nursing and Physical Therapy use more specific professional language; the broader modes ask the AI to infer appropriate expectations from the course and program you describe.

B. Question context

Give the AI enough context to understand who the learners are and what the questions should cover.

Complete the first two fields to build a tailored starter prompt, or skip context to browse the prompt library immediately.
